Meldline

Runs, groups and wild stars. A tile rummy table for 2–4 players.

Solo

Online

How to play
  • Run: 3+ consecutive numbers in one colour. Group: 3–4 of the same number in different colours. A star is wild.
  • Your first play must be your own sets worth 30+ points.
  • After that you can add to, split and rebuild any set on the table, as long as everything is valid when you end your turn.
  • Can't play? Draw a tile. First to empty their rack wins.
  • Drag tiles with a finger. Drag the table to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom.
